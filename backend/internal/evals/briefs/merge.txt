Produce the final review in the output format defined above. Rules for `findings`:

1. **Merge duplicates.** Candidates describing the same defect become ONE finding. Keep the clearest wording and anchor it on the line where the defect is.
2. **Drop nothing.** Every distinct defect a candidate raises appears in the output exactly once, including one only a single reviewer raised. You are merging, not re-reviewing: a candidate you would not have raised yourself still stays. For this pass, this overrides "when in doubt, withhold".
3. **Add nothing.** Do not report a defect no candidate raised.
4. **Evidence stays verbatim.** Every finding still quotes its `evidence` exactly from the diff; reuse a candidate's evidence when it is correct, fix it from the diff when it is not.
